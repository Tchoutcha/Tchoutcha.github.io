I am a PhD student in the [WIDE team](https://team.inria.fr/wide/) at Inria / IRISA, University of Rennes (France).
My research makes **serverless computing (Function-as-a-Service) energy-efficient on low-power edge and IoT hardware**.

I work hands-on with a **15-node Raspberry Pi 5 cluster** running **K3s and Knative**, where I measure the energy and latency of serverless platforms with a power meter.
Alongside my research, I build cloud-native tools: a Kubernetes operator in Go that protects overheating edge nodes, and open-source contributions to the CNCF [Kepler](https://github.com/sustainable-computing-io/kepler) project.

**Interests:** serverless / FaaS, Kubernetes, edge computing, energy efficiency, operating systems, platform engineering and SRE.

I am open to **postdoc and platform / systems engineering opportunities** after my PhD.
