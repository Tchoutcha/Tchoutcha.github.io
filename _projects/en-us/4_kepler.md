**Project:** [Kepler](https://github.com/sustainable-computing-io/kepler) (Kubernetes-based Efficient Power Level Exporter), a CNCF project that estimates the energy used by pods.

**Contribution.** I opened [issue #2743](https://github.com/sustainable-computing-io/kepler/issues/2743), which proposes a power source for Raspberry Pi 5 based on its PMIC (power management chip), and offered to implement and test it on my 15-node Pi 5 cluster.

**Stack:** Go · Kubernetes · Prometheus · Raspberry Pi 5
